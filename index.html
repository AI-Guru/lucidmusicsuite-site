Lucid Music Suite

Produce, perform, plug in

Three music programs that people and AI agents use together: a DAW to make a track, a live instrument to play a set, and a collection of plugins that give both their sound.

  • Native desktop apps
  • macOS · Apple Silicon
  • CLI and MCP in both apps
  • Pre-release

Three products

Each one has its own page and works without the other two.

LucidProducer

The agentic DAW

A digital audio workstation written from scratch in Rust, which people and agents drive with the same commands.

  • Tracks, piano roll, mixer, automation and WAV bounce
  • An MCP server and a CLI
  • Hosts Audio Unit, VST3 and CLAP plugins
LucidProducer page →

LucidPerformer

Live performance with an agent

A clip grid with scenes and macros that you and an agent play together, with every move landing on the beat.

  • Quantized launches and live-coded patterns
  • A Loudness Guard that agents cannot raise
  • A History panel of what was just played
LucidPerformer page →

Lucid Essentials

47 audio plugins

Instruments, effects, MIDI tools and meters with one generated interface, as VST3 and Audio Unit.

  • Synths, physical models, samplers and drums
  • EQ, dynamics, space, modulation and drive
  • Runs in any DAW that loads those formats

How they connect

The two apps share a project format and the same plugins, so work moves between them.

Sound

Essentials plays in both apps

LucidProducer and LucidPerformer both list Lucid Essentials first in their plugin menus. Without it, they fall back to their original built-ins, so projects still play.

Stage to studio

Take a performance home

LucidPerformer exports a performance as a .lucidproj: clip plays become regions, parameter moves become automation, and LucidProducer opens the result.

Agents

The same idea throughout

Both apps come with a CLI and an MCP server, and Lucid Essentials has its own command line tool. An agent can work with all three from a terminal.

Not available for download yet

All three are in pre-release testing on macOS. Public builds will follow.
